Opportunities

PhD positions, research fellowships and internships at CEL Lab.

Current openings

PhD students (1–2 positions)

The lab is looking for one or two PhD students to join from the next admission cycle, to work on urban economics and policy in Indian cities: land and property taxation, urban growth and sprawl, housing and accessibility, or agent-based and spatial modelling of cities. A background in economics, planning, geography or data science and comfort with quantitative work are expected; experience with GIS, R or Python is an advantage. Admission is through the IIT Roorkee PhD programme in the Department of Humanities and Social Sciences. Before applying, write to Dr. Aviral Marwal with a CV and a short note on the questions you would like to work on.

Prospective students and interns

We are always glad to hear from prospective PhD students, master's students looking for a dissertation topic, and interns with a background in economics, planning, geography or data science. Write to us with a short note on your interests and a CV. Funded positions are announced here and on the IIT Roorkee website.
