Work in progress

Papers in preparation. Drafts are circulated once they are ready; write to us if you are working on related questions.

Role of State Policies in Gentrification: A Systematic Literature ReviewHousing affordability, residential segregation and gentrificationAnisha Kathpal and Aviral Marwal · Expected working paper, 2027Gentrification is usually studied as a market process. This review asks what happens when the state is the driver: through zoning, urban renewal, transit and green investment, heritage and tourism promotion, and housing policy. It brings together the international literature on state-led gentrification across the Global North and South, and asks which policies intended to limit displacement have actually worked.
City-Scale Slum Formation Using Agent-Based ModellingHousing affordability, residential segregation and gentrificationShraman Joardar and Aviral Marwal · Expected working paper, 2027Why do slums form where they do? The paper builds an agent-based model in which thousands of households of different incomes search for housing across a city, trading rent against commuting cost, tiring of the search, and doubling up when nothing affordable remains. Informal, multi-family clusters emerge from these decisions rather than being assumed, and the model is used as a testbed for housing and transport policy, beginning with the effect of cheaper public transport on where poor households can afford to live.
