Vacant land tax across Indian cities

What it costs to hold a standard residential plot empty, in 38 cities and 16 states, from 2011 to 2025. Pick a year and a measure, filter by tax design, and click a city for its formula and history.

Figures are medians across the localities sampled in each city (typically 10–15, running from the centre to the edge), for a standard residential plot, in nominal rupees per square metre per year. Only confirmed figures and documented continuity estimates are shown; blanks are gaps, not zeros. Locality-level data are not published. Source: CEL Lab vacant land tax database, 2026.
