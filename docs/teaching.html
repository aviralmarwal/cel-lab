Teaching

Courses at IIT Roorkee

Courses taught by lab members in the Department of Humanities & Social Sciences. Students interested in a dissertation or project in urban economics are welcome to get in touch.

HS 535Autumn · PG / UG elective

Urban Economics

Why cities exist, how they grow, and how land, housing and transport markets shape them. Covers agglomeration, bid-rent and urban form, housing, transport and local public finance, with Indian cases throughout.

HS 659Spring · PG

Spatial Data Analysis

Working with spatial data in R and GIS: geocoding, spatial joins, exploratory spatial data analysis, spatial autocorrelation and spatial regression, applied to urban and regional questions.

HS 508Spring · PG

Institutional Economics

How institutions — property rights, contracts, governance and the state — shape economic outcomes, with applications to land, cities and development.

HS 601Autumn · PG

Welfare Economics

Efficiency, equity and social choice; market failure and public intervention; the normative foundations of policy evaluation.
