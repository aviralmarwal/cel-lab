Welcome to CEL Lab

Aviral Marwal · 22 October 2026

This is a placeholder post, written to show how the blog works. Replace it with the lab’s first real piece.

CEL Lab started at IIT Roorkee in late 2024 with a simple premise: Indian cities generate an enormous amount of data — tax registers, land records, satellite imagery, travel surveys — and very little of it is used to test what actually works. We treat cities as places to experiment and learn.

On this blog, lab members will write short pieces on:

  • what we are finding in the data — property tax systems across states, where cities are expanding, who can reach which services;
  • methods we use and the problems we run into — agent-based models, satellite-derived land cover, synthetic populations;
  • policy debates on Indian cities, from municipal finance to housing affordability.

Posts are written in plain language for planners, officials and students as much as for researchers. Comments and disagreements are welcome by email.
