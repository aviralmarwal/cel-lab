Principal Investigator
AM

Dr. Aviral Marwal

Principal Investigator · Assistant Professor · PhD (Cambridge)

Aviral leads CEL Lab at the Department of Humanities & Social Sciences, IIT Roorkee. His research models urban form, land use and accessibility in Indian cities using geosimulation tools — GIS, agent-based models and neural networks — and examines how land and tax policy shape urban growth. He holds a PhD in Land Economy from the University of Cambridge.
