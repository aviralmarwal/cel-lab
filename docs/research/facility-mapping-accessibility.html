Research / Theme

Facility mapping and accessibility modelling

Where public facilities are, who can reach them, and where new ones would do the most good.

About this theme

Where are schools, health centres, markets and transit stops — and who can actually reach them? We map facilities at fine spatial resolution and model accessibility by walking, cycling and public transport, to identify under-served neighbourhoods and evaluate where new facilities would do the most good.

Issues we tackle

  • Building complete, geocoded facility inventories for Indian cities
  • Measuring access gaps by income, gender and neighbourhood
  • Optimal location of new public facilities
  • Accessibility as an input to service-delivery planning by city governments
