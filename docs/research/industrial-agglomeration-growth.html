Research / Theme

Industrial agglomeration and city economic growth

Where firms and workers cluster, the productivity gains that follow, and what holds city growth back.

About this theme

Cities grow when firms and workers cluster. We study where industries locate within and across Indian cities, the productivity gains from agglomeration, and how misallocation of workers and firms across the urban system holds back growth.

Issues we tackle

  • Location of manufacturing and services within city-regions
  • Agglomeration economies and productivity in Indian cities
  • Spatial misallocation of skill-heterogeneous workers across cities
  • Industrial policy, SEZs and corridor investments

Work in progress

Economic Heterogeneity and Population Distributions of Indian CitiesNikhil Mishra and Aviral Marwal · Expected working paper, 2027Why are some Indian cities compact and others sprawling, and why does the same city change shape over time? The paper argues that the answer lies in what a city's economy does. Using establishment-level employment from the Economic Censuses of 1990, 1998 and 2013, it builds seven indices of the character of each city's economy, such as how tradable, skilled, capital-intensive or centralised its activities are, and relates them to the population density gradient measured from the Global Human Settlement Layer, asking whether shifts in a city's economic make-up can explain shifts in where its people live.
