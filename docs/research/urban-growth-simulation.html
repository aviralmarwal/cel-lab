Research / Theme

Urban growth simulation

Spatially explicit models of where Indian cities will expand, and what policy can change about it.

About this theme

How will Indian cities look in 2040? We build spatially explicit models of urban expansion — cellular automata, agent-based and econometric — calibrated on satellite-derived land cover, census data and infrastructure networks, to simulate where growth will go under alternative policy and infrastructure scenarios.

Issues we tackle

  • Projecting built-up expansion and densification at the city-region scale
  • Scenario testing: ring roads, transit corridors, master-plan land-use rules
  • Peri-urban conversion of agricultural land
  • Validating growth models against observed change
