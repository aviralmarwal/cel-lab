Research / Theme

Municipal finance, property tax and urban sprawl

How cities value and tax land, how tax design shapes sprawl, and how to close the revenue gap.

About this theme

Indian cities raise far less own-revenue than they need. We study how urban local bodies value and tax land and property, how tax design shapes land use and sprawl, and how better data — GIS, satellite footprints, drone surveys — can close the gap between what cities could collect and what they do.

Issues we tackle

  • Property-tax systems across Indian states: valuation, rates, collection
  • Land value taxation and its effect on land use and sprawl
  • Revenue-gap estimation using spatial data
  • Working with municipalities on plot-level pilots

Related projects

  • Assessment of Financial Resources of Local Self-Governing Institutions (PRIs and ULBs) in Uttarakhand (Co-PI)
  • Taxing Vacant Land: A Pan-India Panel Study of Property Tax Discounts and Urban Sprawl in Metropolitan and Tier-2 Cities (PI)
  • Property Tax, Land Records and Urban Growth in Nagar Nigam Roorkee: A Plot-Level Assessment and Policy Options (PI)
