Research / Theme

Commuting behaviour, built environment and neighbourhood typologies

How neighbourhood form shapes the way people travel, and how to classify neighbourhoods for planning.

About this theme

How people travel is shaped by where they live and work and by the form of the neighbourhoods around them. We study commuting patterns and mode choice, link them to density, land-use mix, street networks and transit, and classify neighbourhoods into typologies that help planners target interventions.

Issues we tackle

  • Mode choice and commute distance in Indian cities
  • Built-environment measures (density, diversity, design) at neighbourhood scale
  • Data-driven neighbourhood typologies
  • Travel implications of new housing and job locations

Related projects

  • Population Synthesis for Disaggregated Travel Demand Modelling (Co-PI)
