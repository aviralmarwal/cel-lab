Research / Theme

Housing affordability, residential segregation and gentrification

Who can afford to live where, how households sort across the city, and how neighbourhoods change.

About this theme

Who can afford to live where, and how is that changing? We examine housing prices and rents, the spatial sorting of households by income and social group, and the dynamics of neighbourhood change — including displacement pressures from redevelopment and infrastructure investment.

Issues we tackle

  • Measuring affordability across cities and over time
  • Patterns and drivers of residential segregation
  • Neighbourhood upgrading, redevelopment and displacement
  • Policy levers: housing supply, rental markets, inclusionary rules
