Research / Theme

Land use optimisation for sustainable urbanisation

Allocating scarce urban land among housing, industry, green space and infrastructure.

About this theme

Land is a city's scarcest resource. We develop optimisation and scenario tools that allocate land among housing, industry, green space and infrastructure to meet growth needs while limiting sprawl, protecting ecologically sensitive areas and reducing emissions.

Issues we tackle

  • Multi-objective land-use allocation models
  • Compact vs. dispersed growth trade-offs
  • Green cover, heat and climate resilience in land-use planning
  • Translating optimisation outputs into master-plan inputs
